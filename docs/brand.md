# Brand (Logo Kit v1.0, October 2026)

| Name | Hex | Use |
|---|---|---|
| Earth Brown | #5C3D1E | Primary background/text on light |
| Cream | #FCF6E1 | Light backgrounds, text on brown |
| Sun Yellow | #FBD62B | Horizon bands, highlights |
| Sun Gold | #F4AF16 | Sun, primary buttons |
| Amber | #DC8C13 | Links, accents |

Proportion: 50% Earth Brown, 25% Cream, 25% Yellow/Gold/Amber.

Fonts: **Cinzel** 600-700 (headlines; logo wordmark is outlined Cinzel Bold), **Montserrat** 400-800 (headings, labels in caps with +100 tracking, body). Both bundled in `assets/fonts/`.

Logo files in the theme (`assets/images/brand/`): `-light` versions for Cream/white backgrounds, `-dark` versions for Earth Brown/dark photos. Minimum sizes: horizontal 160 px wide, stacked 120 px, symbol 40 px. Never crop the sunburst rays; on busy photos place the logo on a solid Earth Brown or Cream panel.

The full kit (vector PDF, all PNG sizes, social banners, letterheads) stays in the client's Drive folder.
