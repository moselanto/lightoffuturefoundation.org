## About
Light of Future Foundation is a charitable organisation dedicated to supporting vulnerable communities in Kenya. We focus on assisting widows, widowers, orphans and people living with disabilities through programs that create opportunity and restore dignity.

Our work includes education support, community empowerment, clean water access and youth development. By working directly with communities, we aim to create lasting and sustainable impact.

Leadership: Vice Chairman Nixon Omondo Owino, Secretary Vincent Okoth Okallo, Treasurer Kennedy Oyamo Mumbo. Founder: Francis Ouma Sikuku.

## Our founder
Francis Ouma Sikuku, from Inungo village, Kenya, is a musician, plumber, choir teacher, and counsellor with a deep passion for serving his community. From a young age, he engaged in charity work - visiting widows' homes, helping with cleaning, fetching water, and collecting firewood - which planted the seed for his lifelong commitment to helping others.

In 2016, Francis began formal studies in Social and Community Development, though financial challenges prevented him from completing his diploma. Inspired by his experiences, he dedicated 20% of his earnings from music and plumbing to charitable efforts.

In 2021, Francis began the process of officially registering his work as a charitable organization. After four years of dedication and paperwork, the Light of Future Foundation was formally registered with the NGO Board in May 2025, receiving its official certificate.

"Serving humanity is my passion, and it brings me great joy. It has been a long journey, but with God's grace, we are now able to make a lasting impact in our community."

## Mission / approach
**What we do:** Our mission is to bring hope, opportunity and dignity to vulnerable communities in Kenya by supporting widows, widowers, orphaned children and people living with disabilities.
**How we do it:** Through education support, clean water initiatives, community programs, youth development and spreading the Gospel of Christ, we aim to empower individuals and families to build sustainable and independent futures.

## Programmes
- **Education Support** - Helping vulnerable children and orphans access education and school resources.
- **Community Empowerment** - Supporting widows and struggling families with practical assistance.
- **Clean Water Projects** - Improving access to safe drinking water for local communities.
- **Talent Development** - Encouraging youth talents through sports, music and creative arts.
- **Prison Outreach** - Providing encouragement and guidance through prison visits.

## Success stories
- **Philister** - widow from Inungo village; husband died 1979; lived 45+ years in a grass-thatched house; met in 2023; donors built her a safe home at 85.
- **Elijah** - widower (wife died 2016); found in December 2023 in a coma; taken to hospital; online donors covered medical bills and built him a home; diagnosed with epilepsy; now recovered. Thanks main supporter Kristen.
- **Precious** - young girl in Mathare living with her guardian; floods; foundation paid school fees, uniforms and shoes. **Minor: guardian consent and a pseudonym/no identifying photo required.**
